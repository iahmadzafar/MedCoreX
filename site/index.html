How it's held to standard

Three rules, applied without exception.

RULE 01

The syllabus is the spine

Scope comes from the official syllabus, never from memory or from whichever textbook happened to be open. If it isn't on it, it doesn't earn screen time.

RULE 02

MCQs are the bloodstream

The exam is a hundred multiple-choice questions, so practice runs through everything. Exposure is measured in questions attempted, not pages read.

RULE 03

The error log is sacred

Every wrong answer gets a written record: date, source, your answer, the correct one — and the discriminator you missed. Naming why you missed it is the work.
