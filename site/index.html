                03 · Three rules

                Three rules, no exceptions

                Rule 01

                No syllabus, no session

                Every study block maps to an official syllabus line. If you can't point to the syllabus line a topic belongs to, skip it. No extra memorising, no side topics, no "it might come up".

                Rule 02

                Questions before comfort

                Reading helps you prepare, but answering questions is the practice. A day with no questions done doesn't count as a study day.

                Rule 03

                Every miss gets written

                Don't let a wrong answer pass. Write down, in your own words, the detail that made the right answer right. Skip that step and the question comes back and takes the mark.
