How it’s built

Three rules, applied without exception.

The syllabus is the spine

Scope comes from the official PM&DC document, never from memory or from whichever textbook happened to be open. If it isn’t on the syllabus, it doesn’t earn screen time — however interesting it is.

MCQs are the bloodstream

The exam is a hundred multiple-choice questions, so practice runs through everything. Each drill is a timed set; each mock is a full sitting. Exposure is measured in questions attempted, not pages read.

Error log is sacred

Every wrong answer gets a written record: the date, the source, your answer, the correct one, the discriminator you missed, and the trap type. Reading explanations is not the work. Naming why you missed it is.

Recalled papers rank first

Where a recalled or leaked licensing-exam paper exists, it outranks any mock bank for what it actually tests. Five recalled sessions and their keys are catalogued here, checked question by question.
