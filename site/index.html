The standard

Three rules, no exceptions

Rule 01

No syllabus, no session

Every study block maps to an official syllabus line. If you can't point to the line, you don't study it — no memory, no side quests, no probably-relevant.

Rule 02

Questions before comfort

Pages don't count. Only questions attempted count — end a day without MCQs done and the day didn't happen. Reading prepares; answering is the practice.

Rule 03

Every miss gets written

No wrong answer dies in the review. The discriminator you missed goes into the error log in your own words — or the question returns and takes the mark.
