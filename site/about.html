MedCoreX is a private study library, built for one person's exam preparation and shared with a small group of readers. Every file was built the same way: start from what the exam asks, then write the books, practice questions and papers to match.

Access

You can browse the whole catalogue without an account. Opening a file asks you to sign in with a free account, which keeps your progress with you.

How it's built

Start from the syllabus. Topics come from the official syllabus, not from memory or whichever textbook is open. If a topic isn't on the syllabus, it doesn't get study time.

Practise with questions. The exam is made of multiple-choice questions, so practice is built around them. Progress is measured in questions attempted, not pages read.

Write down every miss. For each wrong answer, record the date, the source, your answer, the correct one, and the detail you missed. Reading the explanation is not enough. Naming why you got it wrong is the work.

What's in it

Complete specialty books, from cardiology to ophthalmology. The main question bank across eighteen subjects. Checked past papers with answer keys. Pattern analysis of themes the exam repeats. Quick-reference sheets for the final review.

Your data

Your account stores your email, your username and your progress. We don't build profiles from what you read, and we don't run advertising. Fonts are served from this site. Hosting sees a standard web request. Your progress stays yours.
