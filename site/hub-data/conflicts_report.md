### Inferior Frontal gyrus of Dominant Hemisphere
*Match:* `superior division of the left middle cerebral artery`

*Reason:* Non-fluent, effortful speech with PRESERVED comprehension after a superior-division MCA stroke is Broca (expressive) aphasia, which localises to the inferior frontal gyrus of the dominant hemisphere. June 2025 keyed inferior temporal lobe — that is Wernicke's area, and would give fluent speech with impaired comprehension. December 2024 is correct.

*Reason:* Normocytic anaemia (MCV 86 fL) with raised ferritin in chronic renal failure is anaemia of chronic disease: treat the EPO deficit and replete iron. June 2025 keyed B12 replacement, which would give macrocytosis. December 2024 is correct.

### Parkinson's disease
*Match:* `expressionless face a resting pill rolling tremor`

*Reason:* Pill-rolling rest tremor, cogwheel rigidity, bradykinesia and a shuffling gait is idiopathic Parkinson disease. June 2025 keyed Huntington disease, which produces chorea and is typically younger-onset. December 2024 is correct.

### Intraductal papilloma
*Match:* `spontaneous bloody discharge from her right nipple`

*Reason:* Spontaneous unilateral bloody nipple discharge with a retroareolar nodule in a 36-year-old is an intraductal papilloma, the commonest benign cause. June 2025 keyed intraductal carcinoma. December 2024 is correct.

### Valine
*Match:* `Maple syrup urine disease`

*Reason:* MSUD is deficiency of the branched-chain alpha-ketoacid dehydrogenase complex, so valine, leucine and isoleucine are implicated. Phenylalanine points to PKU and methionine to homocystinuria, so 'all of the above' cannot be right. December 2025 is wrong.

### Ulnar nerve
*Match:* `The medial cord of the brachial plexus continues as which of the following nerves`

*Reason:* The medial cord's terminal continuation is the ulnar nerve; the medial cutaneous nerve of the forearm and medial pectoral nerve branch from it earlier. As a single-best-answer item the ulnar nerve is correct. FCPS-I repeats this three times with two different keys, so majority voting is unsafe here and the item was adjudicated directly.

### Suppurative
*Match:* `In the early pathological stages of acute appendicitis`

*Reason:* Acute appendicitis characteristically produces suppurative inflammation; gangrenous change is a later complication of vascular compromise, not the early picture.

### Rupture of plantaris tendon
*Match:* `severe pain in the ankle and develops ecchymosis on the second day`

*Reason:* The patient can still stand on the toes, so the Achilles tendon is intact. Sudden ankle pain with delayed bruising and a normal heel-rise is a plantaris rupture (tennis leg).
