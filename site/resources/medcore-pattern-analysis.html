Method & integrity

  • May 2026: the full 200-question paper and 19 source blocks were used. Ten clinical blocks map to Medicine; Anatomy/Physiology blocks split into 6 Anatomy, 9 Physiology, 3 Biochemistry, and 1 Pathology; Forensic/Community blocks split 2/3.
  • Dec 2025: the full 200-question paper and 19 source blocks were used. Ten clinical blocks map to Medicine; Anatomy/Physiology split into 8 Anatomy, 5 Physiology, and 3 Biochemistry; Forensic/Community split 4/4, with the ethics question assigned to Community Medicine under the TOS classification ruling. No keyword matching was used.
  • June 2024 and Dec 2024: recalled questions had no answer sources. Their answer fields were removed rather than imputed as “all A,” so they are excluded from answer-choice analysis.
