6Cardiac tamponadePatient with cancer or TB · severe dyspnoea + distended neck veins rising on inspiration + muffled heart sounds + falling BP
7Infective endocarditisDental procedure or IV drug user weeks earlier · fever for weeks + weight loss + night sweats + splinter haemorrhages / painful finger nodules
8Acute rheumatic feverChild 5–15 years, 2–3 weeks after sore throat · migratory large-joint pain + breathlessness + later uncontrolled limb movements
9Rheumatic mitral stenosisWoman in 30s–40s with childhood sore-throat illness · effort breathlessness + flushing cheeks — first decompensation on pregnancy or any fast heart rate
10Mitral regurgitation (rheumatic)Rheumatic heart disease patient · breathlessness lying flat / on exertion + fatigue — murmur heard at apex radiating to axilla
11Mitral valve prolapseThin young woman · palpitations + occasional sharp chest pain + anxiety-like episodes — heart beat "skipping"
12Degenerative aortic stenosisMan over 65 (or 60s with congenital valve) — progression triad in order: effort breathlessness → chest pain on exertion → sudden faint on exertion
13Hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathyYoung athlete · faints or chest pain during sport (worse standing) — family history of sudden unexplained death
14Dilated cardiomyopathyYoung–middle-aged man, heavy drinker or post-viral · progressive breathlessness lying flat + swelling of ankles + easy fatigue
15Atrial fibrillationElderly or hyperthyroid patient · palpitations with irregular "beat-to-beat chaos" + effort intolerance + no warning spells — may present as sudden stroke
16Paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardiaYoung person with no heart disease · sudden fast pounding heart starting and stopping like a switch (over minutes/hours), anxiety and light-headedness during a run
17Deep vein thrombosisPost-bed-rest / post-surgery / long flight / on pills · one-sided calf pain + swelling + warmth, worse on standing
18Pulmonary embolismPostoperative or post-immobilised patient · sudden breathlessness + sharp chest pain worse on breathing in + fast heart rate, sometimes fainting — calf was swollen days earlier

13Malignant pleural effusionOlder patient, known or unknown cancer · months of gradually increasing breathlessness + dull ache, no fever

17Asbestosis / silicosisYears in asbestos, construction, mining, grinding work · progressive breathlessness + dry cough + finger clubbing (asbestos) years after exposure ends

10DiverticulitisOlder patient · persistent left lower abdominal pain + fever + change in bowel habit — often blotches of blood in stool

13Acute mesenteric ischaemiaElderly atrial-fibrillation patient · pain out of proportion to findings — severe pain, soft abdomen, then rapid collapse with blood in stool
14Ischaemic colitis60+ vascular patient after low-flow episode · sudden left-sided abdominal pain + diarrhoea with fresh blood, low-grade
15Acute pancreatitisAlcoholic or gallstone patient · severe constant boring upper abdominal pain boring through to the back + repeated vomiting easing nothing + worse lying flat — gallstone patient often still, painless-jaundiced before the attack
16Chronic pancreatitisLong-term alcoholic · recurrent episodes of upper abdominal pain boring to the back + steatorrhoea (greasy floating stools) + weight loss + later sugar in blood
17Colorectal cancer50+ with change in stool calibre to ribbon-like + blood mixed in stool + tenesmus (feeling of incomplete evacuation) + iron deficiency in a man
18Pseudomembranous colitisAfter any antibiotic course · profuse watery diarrhoea with mucus, cramping, fever, dehydration
DiseaseSymptom cluster (as the exam presents it)
1Biliary colicObese fertile woman in her 40s ("fat, forty, fertile") · severe steady right-upper pain 30–60 min after fatty meals, radiating to right shoulder, eased after hours — no fever
2Acute cholecystitisSame woman · right-upper pain constant >6 h + fever + vomiting + pain on breathing in under the ribs on the right, tender point below the ribs
3CholedocholithiasisGallstone patient · episodic dark urine + pale stools + itching + mild jaundice WITHOUT fever or rigors — stone sitting quietly in the common duct
4Ascending cholangitisSame patient plus infection · jaundice + rigors and swinging fever + right-upper pain — fever and shaking push it above plain duct blockage
5Hepatitis A / EAdult after street food or contaminated water · 5–10 days of malaise, nausea, aversion to cigarettes then jaundice + passing dark urine — hepatitis E is dangerous in pregnancy
6Hepatitis B (chronic)Often silent · discovered on screening; flares give fatigue + right-upper discomfort + jaundice; late presentation = ascites, variceal bleeding
7Alcoholic hepatitisHeavy drinker · jaundice appearing over days + low-grade fever + tender enlarged liver on the right + anorexia
8Decompensated cirrhosisKnown liver patient · swollen abdomen + oozing bleed from gums, then vomited blood + confusion worse in the evening + yellow eyes
9Spontaneous bacterial peritonitisCirrhotic with ascites · new abdominal pain + fever + worsening confusion, sometimes only confusion with NO pain
10Hepatic encephalopathyCirrhotic · day-night reversal + flapping tremor of outstretched hands + constructional apraxia (cannot draw a star) — trigger = bleed, constipation, infection, sedatives
11Hepatorenal syndromeAdvanced cirrhosis · urine output falling over days with no protein in urine, no blocks, no slumping BP — creatinine climbing in a patient otherwise only cirrhotic
12Wilson's diseaseChild / young adult · clumsiness and slurred speech with strange grimacing + stiffness, or sudden worsening of jaundice in a young cirrhotic + tremor resting-irregular
13HaemochromatosisMan in 40s–50s · grey-bronzed skin + aching joints (2nd/3rd knuckles) + fatigue + sugar in blood + liver enlargement
14Budd-Chiari syndromeYoung woman on pills or clotting disorder · sudden abdominal distension + right-upper pain + tender enlarged liver, no mouth yellowing early
15Amoebic liver abscessMan with amoebic dysentery weeks earlier · swinging fever + night sweats + right-upper pain referred to right shoulder + tender swollen liver
16Hepatocellular carcinomaCirrhotic or hepatitis-B carrier · stable patient deteriorating — weight loss + right-upper ache + rapidly growing liver + sudden abdominal bleed
DiseaseSymptom cluster (as the exam presents it)
1Ischaemic stroke (MCA)Older hypertensive · sudden face droop + arm > leg weakness + slurred or garbled speech on waking
2TIASame patient · identical episode which fully recovers in minutes to <24 h — transient one-eye blindness lasting seconds counts too
3Intracerebral haemorrhageHypertensive · progressive headache + vomiting + hemiparesis evolving over minutes-hours WITH intact consciousness early
4Subarachnoid haemorrhage40s–50s · "worst headache of my life" hitting like a thunderclap during straining + vomiting + neck rigidity, no focal weakness
5Subdural haematomaElderly or alcoholic after trivial stumble (days–weeks earlier) · slowly fluctuating drowsiness + confusion + headache, one pupil later dilating
6Extradural haematomaYoung man hit on the temple · brief blackout, then lucid interval of hours, then rapid dilating pupil + weakness on the opposite side + vomiting
7Bacterial meningitisYoung adult / child · fever + severe headache + vomiting + neck stiffness + photophobia; child may show only fever + lethargy + refusal to feed + high-pitched cry, bulging fontanelle if <18 months
8HSV encephalitisAdult · fever + confusion behaving bizarrelly + olfactory/smell hallucinations or unpleasant drinking of previously tolerated food — often mis-sent home
9Multiple sclerosisYoung woman · blurred vision in one eye with pain moving the eye + numb patches + electric shock down the back on neck bending, relapsing and remitting
10Guillain-Barré syndromeYoung adult 2–4 weeks after diarrhoea or flu · symmetric weakness climbing legs → arms over days + absent reflexes + backache; breathing effort is the danger
11Myasthenia gravisYoung woman / older man · drooping eyelids and double vision worsening through the day + difficulty chewing meat at dinner but fine at breakfast — fatigue improves with rest
12Parkinson's diseaseMan over 60 · pill-rolling finger tremor at rest + smaller handwriting + shuffle + staring face, all beginning on one side
13Huntington's diseaseMan in 30s–40s · facial grimacing + writhingpurposeless limb movements + personality change and irritability, parent died the same way
14Motor neuron disease (ALS)Man over 50 · wasted hand muscles + jumping twitches + slurred speech + stumbling, NO sensory loss, NO eye or bladder involvement
15Bell's palsyOvernight · one-sided whole-half face droop + inability to wrinkle the forehead or close the eye + taste blunted on the front tongue; forehead sparing = stroke, not Bell's
16Trigeminal neuralgiaOver 50 · electric-shock jabs on one side of the face from eating, washing, cold wind; completely painless between attacks, never crosses midline
17Migraine vs clusterYoung woman · one-sided throbbing headache preceded by flashing lights, nausea, light intolerance, sleep fixes it — vs a man smoker: sudden eye-tearing one-sided knife-stab behind the eye at the same clock time nightly for weeks
18Wernicke's encephalopathyAlcoholic or vomiting post-op young man · confusion + walking like a sailor + darting eye movements/jerks — never wait for the classic three; give the vitamin first
DiseaseSymptom cluster (as the exam presents it)
1Schizophrenia (first episode)Young adult 18–25 · months of social retreat and strange preoccupations, then clear auditory commentary voices + fixed false beliefs + flattened affect
2Acute psychotic maniaYoung adult · three sleepless nights + grandiose pressure speech + spending sprees + sexual disinhibition
3Bipolar depressionSame patient · opposite phase: retarded movement + guilt-heavy hopelessness + early-morning waking in low mood
4Major depressive disorderMiddle-aged woman · >2 weeks of pervasive disinterest + fatigue + early-morning waking + appetite drop + worthlessness; ask about plans to harm self
5Persistent depressive disorderYears of "always been like this" mildly-flat mood, never a clear episode — two years minus brief relief
6Panic disorderYoung woman · sudden 10-minute surge of racing heart + choking + dread of dying or going mad, peaks at 10 min then subsides, leaving dread of the next one
7Generalised anxietyYears of constant worry about many things + restlessness + poor sleep + tension headaches + irritable bowel
8PTSDAfter a road accident or violence witnessed · months of nightmares/flashbacks of the event + avoiding that junction/shop/vehicle + constant startle + irritability
9OCDYoung adult · hours spent washing, checking, praying counting; knows it is irrational, distressing, but cannot stop — eating time stealing the day
10Anorexia nervosaAdolescent girl · starvation-thin yet insists she is fat + exercises compulsively + period stops + soft hair on the arms + cold intolerance + bradycardia
11Bulimia nervosaNormal-weight young woman · binge-empties in secret, then vomiting or laxative abuse + eroded enamel + swollen parotids + fresh stretch-scars on knuckles
12Alcohol withdrawalDrinker 6–48 h after last drink · coarse tremor + sweating + anxiety, then 24–72 h: hallucinations, confusion, seizures; later vivid visual hallucinations of small animals, fully awake
13Delirium vs dementia— sudden onset + fluctuating consciousness + visual hallucinations + worse at night = delirium; years-slow decline with clear consciousness = dementia
14Somatic symptom / illness anxietyMultiple doctors later, no organic findings fit the fixed conviction of disease; multiple organ complaints for years starting before 30
15Conversion (functional neuro) disorderAfter an emotional upset · weakness/blindness/seizure that fails anatomically — no dissonance (patient calm), reflexes intact, imaging clean
16Opioid overdose / withdrawalOverdose: pinpoint pupils + slowed breathing + unconsciousness — withdrawal: gooseflesh + runny eyes + cramps + diarrhoea + dilated pupils + yawning
DiseaseSymptom cluster (as the exam presents it)
1Diabetic ketoacidosisYoung diabetic missing insulin or first presentation · 1–2 days of rapid laboured breathing + fruit-smelling breath + vomiting + abdominal pain + drowsiness
2Hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic stateElderly bed-bound type-2 · days to weeks of polyuria + confusion or coma, NO quick breathing, NO fruit breath — serum syrup-high
3Type 1 diabetes (first presentation)Child/young adult · weeks of drinking gallons + urinating round the clock + weight falling despite eating + bedwetting returning
4Type 2 diabetesOverweight 40s–50s with family history · often silent; fatigue + recurrent skin/genital infections + slow-healing wounds + blurred vision after meals
5HypoglycaemiaDiabetic on insulin / sulfonylurea · trembling + cold sweat + hunger, coming on as intoxication; unexplained episodes → alcohol-like behaviour at wrong times = insulinoma
6Graves' diseaseYoung woman · weight loss with large appetite + palpitations + heat intolerance + eye prominence + neck swelling + pretibial flesh swelling
7Toxic multinodular goitreOlder woman with long-standing neck swelling · suddenly hyperthyroid (— no eye signs)
8Thyroid stormHyperthyroid after infection/surgery · delirium + fever >40 + tachyarrhythmia + vomiting = an emergency cluster
9HypothyroidismMiddle-aged woman · weight gain with poor appetite + cold intolerance + constipation + dry skin + hoarse voice + heavy periods + slow reflex relaxation
10Myxoedema comaLong-standing hypothyroid stopped medication, elderly in winter · hypothermia + coma + slow breathing + facial puffiness
11Subacute (de Quervain's) thyroiditisAfter viral sore throat · painful neck swelling moving to jaw + transient hyperthyroidism then swing to low
12Primary hyperparathyroidismMan/woman over 50 on routine chemistry · "moans, stones, groans": fatigue/depression + recurrent kidney stones + bone aches + constipation + peptic ulcer
13Cushing syndromeMiddle-aged or young · moon face + central fat with thin limbs + purple stretch marks + easy bruising + high BP + sugar; woman also stalls cycles + body-hair excess
14Addison's disease / adrenal crisisYoung woman · weeks of fatigue + salt craving + nausea + weight loss + darkening of skin creases/nipples; crisis on infection/stop steroid: vomiting + collapse + low BP unresponsive to drips
15Pheochromocytoma50s patient · paroxysmal attacks: pounding headache + drenching sweats + palpitations + pale pallor with BP spiking — between attacks completely asymptomatic
16Primary hyperaldosteronismYoung-middle-aged hypertensive with potassium LOW and no clear other cause — muscle cramps + numbness + palpitations + polyuria
17AcromegalyAdult · rings and shoes tightening over months + enlarged hands/feet/jaw + sweating + headaches + snoring, plus carpal-tunnel symptoms
18ProlactinomaYoung woman · absent or absent periods + milk leaking from breasts without pregnancy + headaches; man: headaches + vision loss + lost libido
