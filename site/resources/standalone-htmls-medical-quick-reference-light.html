SIRS · Sepsis · Septic Shock
SIRS — ≥2 Criteria
  • Temp >38°C or <36°C
  • HR >90 bpm
  • RR >20/min or PaCO₂ <32 mmHg
  • WBC >12,000 or <4,000 /mm³
    or Bands >10%
Sepsis
SOFA Score ≥2 + suspected/confirmed infection
SOFA Components
PaO₂/FiO₂<400
Platelets<150
Bilirubin>1.2 mg/dL
MAP<70 mmHg
GCS<15
Creatinine>1.2 mg/dL
Each scored 0–4. SOFA ≥2 + infection = Sepsis.
Septic Shock
Sepsis + persistent hypotension requiring vasopressors + MAP ≥65 mmHg + Lactate >2 mmol/L despite adequate fluid resuscitation
ECG Axis Interpretation
Normal: −30° to +90°
LAD: −30° to −90°
RAD: +90° to +180°
Extreme: ±180° to −90°
QUICK CHECK
Lead I ↑ + aVF ↑ → Normal
Lead I ↑ + aVF ↓ → LAD
Lead I ↓ + aVF ↑ → RAD
Lead I ↓ + aVF ↓ → Extreme
