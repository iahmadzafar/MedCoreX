Questions, answered plainly

The small print of belonging to the shelf — plainly said.

What is MedLib X?

A private study library — seven groups wide: complete system books, a merged question bank, recalled past-paper sessions with verified keys, exam intelligence, and quick-reference sheets. Everything is open — walk in and read.

What's actually in it?

Seven shelves: core books, the merged test series, past papers with keys, the intelligence layer that says what the next paper will test, quick references, interactive case practice, and ten complete system books from cardiology to ophthalmology.

Do I need an account?

No. Accounts were part of an earlier plan; for now every file on the shelf is open to anyone who walks in.

Does MedLib X track me or show ads?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no third-party scripts and no advertising anywhere in the house. Read the longer version on the About page.

Can I share the files or use them elsewhere?

The files are licensed for your own personal study — read them anywhere you like, pass the link rather than the files themselves. This house stays open for anyone you send here.

How current is the material?

Kept current and exact is part of the house standard. Recalled sessions sit beside their verified keys, corrected keys are marked as such, and anything superseded is pulled from the shelf rather than left smelling stale.

Something's broken or a file looks wrong.

Tell the librarian — bugs get fixed before furniture gets replaced. Say which file, which group, and what you saw; the shelf is maintained with patience on purpose.
